Chapter IV — Heritage

A lineage of quiet
excellence.

2014

The Founding

A small atelier opens on Rue de Seine, Paris. Three artisans, one vision: to craft objects worthy of a lifetime.

2017

First Collection

The debut collection, "Silences," is presented at Palais de Tokyo. Twelve pieces. Twelve stories. An immediate quiet sensation.

2020

Global Ateliers

Ateliers open in Milan, Kyoto, and New York. Each preserves the intimate scale of the original — never more than seven artisans.

2023

The Heritage Archive

Launch of the permanent archive — a living museum of every piece ever conceived, accessible by private appointment.

2026

Autumn Edition

The seventh volume. A meditation on gold, shadow, and the enduring language of craft. Presented this season, worldwide.
